## 共通様式（⑥Style）—— 手彩色の古地図（荒い紙・手書き抽象）

hakuchizu の設計が既に持つ視覚様式（二つの描画則）に、3つのスタイル参照の**技術語彙**を融合したもの。

- **元様式（参照）**: スケッチ風（示唆して描き切らない・不完全なエッジ）＋荒い紙・手書き抽象（粗い温かいオフホワイト紙・グアッシュ的ストローク・ドライブラシ・紙の露出・大きな余白・ミニマル）。
- **転調（hakuchizu 固有）**: パレットを新造せず、作品の**手彩色の古地図**（朱・群青・黄土・金彩の微光）に固定。忘れられた世界＝**純白＋薄い鉛筆グリッド**、境界＝**濡れたインクの縁**。参照ファイルの `[COLOR PALETTE]` をこの4色＋紙の白＋ほぼ黒のチャコール（鉛筆）で埋める。

### 忠実アンカー（fidelity anchors）
rough slightly-textured warm off-white uncoated paper (paper as the dominant field) · broad opaque gouache-like strokes mixed with subtle transparent washes · dry-brush marks · uneven pigment density · irregular edges · small exposed-paper areas · vermilion 朱 / ultramarine 群青 / yellow ochre 黄土 / faint gold-leaf glint 金彩 · thin pencil grid lines for the forgotten white · wet ink edge where color meets white · incomplete forms dissolving into paper · large quiet negative space · asymmetrical balance · flat and paper-based (no dramatic lighting, no spatial illusion) · minimal, tactile, poetic, handmade, quiet, slightly melancholic.

### ネガティブ（⑦Negative）
`not photorealistic, no digital polish, no vector-like edges, no glossy rendering, no decorative gradients, no excessive detail, no typography, no frame, no watermark, no signature, no dramatic lighting`

> **唯一の例外**: 娘の顔の四角（スチル2）だけは、手彩色ではなく**写真的な質感**にする（様式の破れ＝世界法則の穴）。ただし顔そのものはくっきり描かず、ぼけた写真のような抑制に留める。他のすべては手彩色・非写実。

### 人物アンカー（④Keep consistent）
- **灯**: 中年の女。地味な服。**基本は背中か輪郭、白くなる手**で示す（顔はほとんど出さない）。輪郭の端が白く透ける。
- **澪（娘）**: 顔は「**写真的な質感の四角**」としてだけ現れる（くっきり描かない）。手彩色の世界の様式に従わない。

## スチル1 — 暖簾（第1章・色が白を喰い戻す）

**選定理由**: 「色が白を喰い戻す」を一点に凝縮。白い通り（忘れられた世界）で、暖簾だけが朱に戻る——色が白を喰う最初の一撃を、一枚の暖簾に託す。

```
A hand-colored old map painted on rough warm off-white paper: a nearly blank street drawn only in
thin pencil grid lines, where a single shop noren has just regained its color — a broad opaque
gouache stroke of vermilion red, the only color in the frame, its wet edge bleeding slightly into
the white as if the color is eating the blankness back. A woman's faintly whitening hand reaches
in from the lower edge. Large quiet negative space, exposed paper, dry-brush marks, uneven
pigment density, imperfect edges, no dramatic lighting, flat and paper-based, minimal, poetic,
slightly melancholic. Not photorealistic, no digital polish, no vector-like edges, no glossy
rendering, no decorative gradients, no excessive detail, no typography, no frame, no watermark.
```

## スチル2 — 娘の顔の四角（第4章・唯一の写真）

**選定理由**: 手彩色の世界の中で一点だけ、写真的な質感の四角（娘の顔）が残る。灯が唯一差し出さない記憶＝世界の穴。ただし顔はくっきり描きすぎず、ぼけた写真のような抑制で浮かべる——様式の破れは「写真的な質感」であって、顔そのものの鮮明さではない。

```
A hand-colored old map of a small town painted on rough warm off-white paper, its streets and
roofs built of broad gouache strokes in vermilion red, ultramarine blue, and yellow ochre with a
faint gold-leaf glint, dry-brush texture and exposed paper throughout. At its center floats one
small square that breaks the painting: a quiet, faded photographic likeness of a girl's face,
soft and restrained, its rectangular edges clean against the brushwork, like a single photograph
gone soft — a memory held too tightly to be painted over, the one thing in the frame that does
not dissolve into the paper. The face is suggested rather than sharply rendered, half-legible.
The painted forms around it remain incomplete and dissolve into the paper. Flat, paper-based, no
dramatic lighting, quiet negative space, minimal, melancholic. Not photorealistic elsewhere, no
digital polish, no vector-like edges, no glossy rendering, no decorative gradients, no excessive
detail, no typography, no frame, no watermark.
```

## スチル3 — 白紙の町（第5章・忘れられた世界）

**選定理由**: 忘れられた世界＝純白＋鉛筆グリッド。白紙に戻った町を、色を一切使わず、**町の全貌が分かる程度まで**鉛筆グリッドで描く（薄すぎて何も見えない、ではなく、構造が読める程度に）。復元される前の「忘れられた」の全景。

```
A forgotten town drawn on rough warm off-white paper entirely in thin graphite pencil, no color
anywhere: a fine architectural grid of pencil lines maps out the whole town — a lattice of
streets, the lean outlines of many houses and shopfronts, a bridge, a distant shrine gate — all
rendered as precise but faint linework with a visible grid underneath, complete but colorless,
an uncolored map waiting to be painted. Large areas of white paper stay open between the lines.
Flat, paper-based, no dramatic lighting, no spatial illusion, minimal, quiet, slightly
melancholic. Not photorealistic, no digital polish, no vector-like edges, no glossy rendering,
no decorative gradients, no excessive detail, no typography, no frame, no watermark.
```

## スチル4 — 代償（第8章・自己消滅）

**選定理由**: 零和の取引を一枚に。街が色づくほど、灯の手が白く透ける——「歩く＝色が白を喰う＝灯が白くなる」を、色づく街と白くなる手の同一のジェスチャーで示す。

```
A hand-colored old map on rough warm off-white paper showing the cost of remembering: in the
foreground, a woman's hand and forearm are fading to paper-white, turning transparent at the
edges as if the pigment is being drawn out of her, while behind her the street blooms fully with
broad gouache strokes of vermilion red, ultramarine blue, yellow ochre, and a gold-leaf glint.
The color in the town and the draining white of the hand are the same gesture. Dry-brush marks,
uneven pigment density, exposed paper, wet ink edges, flat and paper-based, no dramatic lighting,
quiet negative space, minimal, melancholic. Not photorealistic, no digital polish, no vector-like
edges, no glossy rendering, no decorative gradients, no excessive detail, no typography, no
frame, no watermark.
```

## スチル5 — 逆転・墓（第10章・超出×必然）

**選定理由**: 幹の答え。灯が娘を場所にしたのではなく、娘が灯を墓に変えていた。色づいた町（澪の墓）の中央で、灯の白い影が喰われ、墓になる——悼む者＝悼まれる者を、白い影として見せる。

```
A hand-colored old map of a town on rough warm off-white paper, saturated everywhere with broad
gouache strokes of vermilion, ultramarine, yellow ochre, and gold leaf — except at its center,
where a woman's figure stands as a white void, her silhouette the only unpainted shape, its edges
blurred and bleeding as the surrounding color presses in and absorbs her, the town's paint eating
her white like a grave closing. Dry-brush marks, uneven pigment density, exposed paper, wet ink
edges, flat and paper-based, no dramatic lighting, large quiet negative space, minimal, poetic,
slightly melancholic. Not photorealistic, no digital polish, no vector-like edges, no glossy
rendering, no decorative gradients, no excessive detail, no typography, no frame, no watermark.
```

## スチル6 — 白い四角（第12章・頂点→領収書）

**選定理由**: 塗りきった町の中央に、人一人ぶんの白い四角が残る。消失でも解放でも救済でも裁きでもない、取引の領収書。母と娘が同じ形になった場所を、絵の具の盛り上がりの海に浮かぶ一点の紙の白として閉じる。

```
A hand-colored old map of a small town on rough warm off-white paper, densely painted everywhere
with broad gouache strokes of vermilion red, ultramarine blue, yellow ochre, and a faint gold-leaf
glint, raised and thick — and at its exact center, one clean person-sized white square, pure
exposed paper with no paint at all, the only untouched shape in the map, as bright and empty as a
receipt. The painted edges around the square stop abruptly at a wet ink line. Flat, paper-based,
no dramatic lighting, no spatial illusion, quiet negative space, minimal, tactile, handmade,
slightly melancholic. Not photorealistic, no digital polish, no vector-like edges, no glossy
rendering, no decorative gradients, no excessive detail, no typography, no frame, no watermark.
```

## 補足

- **様式の由来**: 3つの参照ファイル（スケッチ風＝示唆して描き切らない・不完全なエッジ／手書き抽象＝粗い紙・グアッシュ・ドライブラシ・余白・非写実）を、hakuchizu が**既に持つ**手彩色の古地図（朱・群青・黄土・金彩＋純白＋鉛筆グリッド＋濡れたインクの縁）に融合。参照ファイルはパレットを新造するのでなく、その描き方（技術語彙）を与える。
- **「非写実」の唯一の例外**: 娘の顔の四角だけが写真的な質感（様式の破れ）。ただし顔はくっきり描かず、ぼけた写真のような抑制で留め、世界の穴を「鮮明な顔」ではなく「様式の違う質感」として可視化する。
