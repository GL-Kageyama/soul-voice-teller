# 2 · The Tea — scene

> Skill: `/scene-writer` · the most loaded scene, written deep · Input: design (scene table · narration · prose style) + demo persona
> Designed empty space first: never say who the second cup is for; never say why; never name what he feels. What the scene hands over, unspoken, is the second cup — poured every day, poured out every day.

The kettle had gone cold on the stove.

He filled it and set it back. The water came to a boil. Steam rose and wet the glass.

He took two cups down from the shelf. One he set at his place. One he set across the table.

He poured. The tea darkened in both cups.

He sat and drank from his own. The other he did not look at.

The steam from the second cup thinned, then stopped. The tea cooled.

After a while he set his cup down. He took the second cup to the sink and tipped it out, slowly, so it did not splash. He rinsed it and set it on the shelf beside his own.

He dried his hands and hung the cloth and sat down again.

The window held the flat water. The chair across the table stayed empty.
