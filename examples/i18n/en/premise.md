# premise — the work's entrance

> Skill: `/premise` · Input: seed "A lighthouse keeper fears the sea, yet waits for the only guest who comes by sea" + demo persona (+ no store)

## Logline
A keeper who fears the sea keeps a light and waits for the one guest who could only come by sea.

## The core question
Why does a person keep waiting for something that never comes? (held against the persona's soul-story)

## Theme
Waiting, and what was lost. He fears the water, yet the water is the only road a guest could take — fear and waiting share one place.

## The genre promise
A quiet short piece. No event arrives. What is read is the time of waiting, and the light moving over it.

## Direction of prose style
Short sentences. Breathe at the period. Avoid adjectives; write with nouns and verbs. Feeling is shown in action and silence, never named (the persona's voice & brushwork).

## Reader image & promise
For a reader who has waited. Without explaining, leave the space that calls up the reader's own memory of waiting. Promise a quiet displacement, nothing more.
