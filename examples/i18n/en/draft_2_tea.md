# 2 · The Tea

> Skill: `/fast-draft` · scene 2 of 3 · Input: design (scene table) + demo persona

Noon. The sea lay flat. Nothing moved on it.

He filled the kettle and set it on the stove. When it whistled he poured two cups.

One he set at his place. The other he set across the table.

He drank his own. The second cup cooled.

When the tea was cold, he carried it to the sink and poured it out. He rinsed the cup and set it back on the shelf.

Then he sat again and looked at the water. The chair across the table stayed empty.
