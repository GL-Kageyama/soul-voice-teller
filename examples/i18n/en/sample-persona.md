# persona (demo sample)

> Fictional demo persona, not a real writer's experience.

## Soul-story (why I write)
Drawn back again and again to memories of the sea and the waterside. Each time I write, "waiting" and "what was lost" return. The unsettled question: why does a person keep waiting for something that never comes?

## Aesthetic sense (what makes good prose)
Good prose leaves the negative space unfilled. Prose that over-explains, or names the emotion, is bad. Quiet actions (pouring tea, smoothing a footprint) carry the feeling.

## Voice & brushwork (how I write)
Short sentences. Breathe at the period. Avoid adjectives; write with nouns and verbs.
- Concrete-sentence anchor: "The tea poured for the guest who never comes grows cold."

## Forbidden moves (what I don't write)
Never write "sad" or "lonely". Don't wrap up the theme at the ending. Don't hide behind metaphor. Don't drift into sentimentality.

## Subject-matter preference (what draws me)
The sea, the waterside, lighthouses. Quiet solitude. Dusk and dawn. People who don't speak, and people who wait.
