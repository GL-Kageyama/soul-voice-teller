# series-bible — The Keeper

> Skill: `/series-bible` · A ledger that centrally manages setting · foreshadowing · serialization for the work.

## Setting record (the dictionary)

- **Work**: "The Keeper" — a quiet short piece; a lighthouse keeper on a rock who fears the sea and waits for the only guest who could come by sea.
- **The keeper**: middle-aged, unnamed. Wound: someone lost to the sea (never told). Belief: the light must not go out. He almost never speaks.
- **The guest**: unnamed; never arrives. Who it is is never told.
- **The lost one**: unnamed; never explained; the felt absence behind everything.
- **The rock and the tower**: a lighthouse on a small rock offshore; the sea on all sides. Interior: the stairs, the room with the lens, the room below with the table and two chairs.
- **The lamp**: oil, a wick, a hand-cleaned lens. Lit at dusk, tended through the night. The level of technology is fixed here (no electricity, no machinery).
- **The supply boat**: comes by sea on a schedule never stated; delivers oil and food; it is not the guest.
- **The mainland**: too far to see; present only as where the supply boat comes from.
- **The second cup**: the keeper's private rite — tea poured for two, the second cup left to cool and then poured out, every day.
- **Era**: unnamed (`?`), implied pre-electrification (roughly mid-19th to early-20th century).

## Serialization record

- **Boundary of this installment**: three days — or three pieces of one unending day — and no event arrives. The installment is complete in itself.
- **Promise for next time**: the waiting does not end; nothing is closed. If the work continues, the next installment begins at the same water's edge, with the same second cup.
- **Consistency with the previous**: n/a (first installment). The rules to keep: no name for the lost one, no event that resolves the waiting, the light must not go out.
- **Promise of the series**: a series of quiet returns to the same rock, each one a different weather, the same waiting.
