# 2 · The Tea — prose

> Skill: `/prose` · polished for rhythm · sense · voice · Input: scene 2 + design (prose style) + demo persona

The kettle had gone cold on the stove.

He filled it. He set it back. The water climbed to a boil; the steam rose and wet the glass.

Two cups, down from the shelf. One at his place. One across the table.

He poured. The tea darkened in both. The steam over the second cup thinned and stopped. The tea cooled.

He drank his own without hurrying. When it was gone he carried the second cup to the sink and tipped it out, slowly, so the tea ran down the basin in a thin thread and did not splash. He rinsed the cup and set it on the shelf beside his own.

He dried his hands and hung the cloth and sat down again.

The window held the flat water. The chair across the table stayed empty.
