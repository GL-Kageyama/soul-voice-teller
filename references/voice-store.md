# The Store of the Soul's Voice (voice-ledger) — store the writer's soul-voice, and read it back

> The **persistence** of the design principle "the inner voice is the source of everything; introspect and listen."
> The voices heard in [introspection.md](introspection.md) (A the writer's setting / B ideation / C design / D the draft loop) and
> the departures · the moments the writing took off, are stored across works, and read back for later ideation · escaping a stall · updating writer-persona.

---

## 1. Why store it

- **The voice lives in context.** Don't let the moment you heard it slip. Introspection vanishes per session — unless you record it, the voice does not remain.
- **If you record it, it connects later.** A voice heard in another work answers a new question in an unexpected form. The count of stored seeds is the count of flashes.
- **The soul-story deepens with every work you write.** The store is the record of that deepening, and the **material for updating** writer-persona (the present-tense setting).
- **A ledger no one else could keep.** Written from the stored voice, the prose becomes prose no one else could write.

## 2. What to store (six kinds of voice)

| Kind | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The voice of ideation | B questions · a flash | a fragment heard as "this material answers my recurring question this way" |
| The voice of design | C questions · a record of a drift | where the design drifted from the promise and deepened, or a choice fixed as "this writer's" |
| The voice of the draft | D questions · a record of a departure | where the character left the plan and began to move · their line |
| The moment the writing took off | flow | the conditions under which emergence happened (material · constraint · empty space). Record **the conditions themselves** as a clue for reproducing it |
| The moment you touched a forbidden move | A-4 | almost broke it · broke it. Whether it became depth or breakage |
| The resonance of evaluation | reader reaction | what resonated · what didn't, in external evaluation. Without being swept along, keep only the sound |

## 3. When to store it (evoke, do not force)

- **Only when a voice is heard** (D-4). On days nothing is heard, don't force a line; don't add empty lines to the store.
- When a departure is judged to "deepen".
- When answering a question, it became "this is it".
- When the writing took off · when it went dry (to make it a water source later).
- When the question forms (A/B/C/D) heard an answer, store it on the spot.

## 4. How to store it (the form of one entry)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Date · work · scene | when · in which work · where it was heard |
| The voice heard | **verbatim. Do not summarize — the voice dies in summary** |
| Context (which question it answered) | quote the question form (A/B/C/D) so the same question can re-evoke it |
| Used / unused | the transition from flash to writing. A mark for connecting later |

**Example of the form**

```
- [x] 2026-08-14 / The Man Walking at Night / chapter 2 · the climactic line
  voice: this man doesn't "not apologize" — he "cannot apologize". Because if he apologizes, something ends.
  context: D-1 (did this line come from the plan's demand, or from the character himself?)
  used: the climax of chapter 2. The resonance of evaluation is here too (originality was high).
```

## 5. Reading it back (do not end at storing)

The store exists not only to store but to **read**. A ledger that is never read becomes a dumping ground.

- **At the start of ideation** (premise's B questions) — call the previous work's voice into the new question
- **When updating writer-persona** — read the accumulated voices as "where the soul-story went", and revise the setting
- **At a stall** — the water source when you cannot write
- **When fast-draft goes dry** (D-4, nothing heard → fall back to following the plan) — before falling back, listen to the store

## 6. The relation between the store and writer-persona

- `writer-persona` = **fixing the voice** (present-tense setting · portrait). Fixed by A questions (dialogue with the human), kept permanent.
- `voice-ledger` = **storing the voice** (past-tense voice · diary). Accumulates the voices heard in A/B/C/D questions.
- As the store accumulates, the persona is **updated** (deepening of the soul-story · revision of forbidden moves · maturing of the voice).
- The two are separate files: persona is "I write like this", the store is "I have written like this".
- The soul-story and the voice are **the human's (user's)**. The AI is a mirror that reflects · stores · returns them, not the author.

## 7. The nature of its skill-ification

- **Recording · ledgers are easy to externalize** → suited to being a skill.
- But the choice of "**what counts as a voice**" requires judgment (it is observation, not judgment · introspection §0). The store itself **does not judge** — it stores, and reads. The choice of voice is made by the question-asking skills (persona / premise / plot-design / fast-draft).
