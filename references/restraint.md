# The Aesthetic of Restraint — the Master Lever of the Soul

> Position: what moves the soul is the manner of writing = restraint. Built into fast-draft as its **output specification**.

---

## 1. What it is

**What moves the soul (emotional power) is not the particularity of the design, but the "manner" of writing = restraint.**

- The particularity of the design moves only particularity (the means), not the soul.
- Restraint in the manner of writing moves **both** the soul (the end) and particularity (the means). → **the master lever**.

## 2. The five clauses of the aesthetic of restraint (fast-draft's output specification)

1. **Do not explain emotion** — do not write "I was sad", "I was glad". Show it through action and silence.
2. **Do not name it** — do not state the theme or emotion in words. Do not obstruct the reader from feeling it.
3. **Do not put a moral or summary at the ending** — do not crush the reader's displacement with a "wrap-up". Do not fold it up in the last line; leave it.
4. **Leave negative space for the reader** — an emptiness that invites the reader's own grief and questions. Leave it without pressing.
5. **Do not write the persona's soul-story as the reason** — the wound, the unsettled question, are used only as the source behind the work; do not write "years ago…" into the draft. **Hold the source, do not speak the source.**

## 3. "Explanation, naming, summary" are a double harm

- **Fabricated sentiment** — the more you explain an emotion, the further you get from the real thing.
- **Mediocrity** — "an explanation anyone could write" becomes no one's prose.

## 4. Restraint is necessary, not sufficient

```
soul = true emotion (SOURCE) × the story earns the emotion (SHAPE) × delivered with restraint (MANNER)
```

- Restraint (MANNER) needs a **source** for "**what** it restrains". Without a "true emotion" to restrain, restraint is mere emptiness (restraint of nothing).
- The source is **SOURCE (checking = the writer's real experience)**. This is a design problem for writer-persona ([introspection.md](introspection.md) chapter A).
- This "source" is not something to narrate in the draft as a device for tears (turning it into a device for tears erodes MANNER — §6).
- The earning (SHAPE = causality · fall · lack) is also needed separately.

## 5. Application in fast-draft

- Restraint is fast-draft's **output specification**. It is not an instruction before writing, but a criterion for inspecting what you have written (whether "explanation · naming · summary" has slipped in, whether the negative space has been crushed).
- Restraint and introspection are **compatible**: introspection allows the departure (follows the voice), restraint tightens the delivery (does not break it). Departing does not license you to start explaining.
- Forbidden moves (persona A-4) and restraint overlap but are distinct: forbidden moves are "what this writer will not write"; restraint is "a universal law of delivery for carrying the soul".

## 6. Hold the source, do not speak the source (the boundary of SOURCE and MANNER)

- **What lowers the soul is not "speaking the source" but "speaking the source as a device for tears"**. Touching the source once, as fact, does not break the soul. Using the source as a device to induce tears is the harm.
- "Hold the source, do not speak the source" is a **safety valve** against turning the source into a device for tears. In substance, clause 4 (do not name it) already covers it, but it is kept as an explicit warning.
