# Radical revision toward entertainment

## ― What to do when the vessel itself forbids the axis ―

The six axes ([six-response-axes.md](six-response-axes.md)) say **which** reaction is weak and **how** to fill it — without breaking expression and restraint. This file answers the case that comes *before* both: **the vessel (器) of the existing draft cannot carry the axis at all**, and no amount of filling will change that.

Measured from one case: `habits` → `examples/habits-comedy/` (第1話「消さなかった一行」 · 第2話「書く前の口」, 2026-10-15). The 第1話 draft was accepted by the author.

**No speculation.** Every clause carries its source (a measurement · a ruling · a commit). A clause without a source is not a clause.

## 0. The spine — humor sits in the **second beat**, not in the **voice**

**A gap (ズレ) is "build a prediction, then break it". That is two beats.**

- **Humor on the vocabulary side** — a swapped term, an excess of precision — can throw **one beat only**.
- **Measured.** `habits` ruled that humor is not an axis but lives in the voice (`habits/構想/design.md`:138), and the vocabulary side did carry it — `01-01:135` 「短い。短い針がいちばん速い。」 (a swapped term) and `01-01:153` 「鳴るところは三つである。」 (excess precision). **It landed in 1 scene out of 189.**
- **Why it cannot land is measured on the `habits` side.** `habits/研ぎ/台帳を検める.py`:274 forbids `inner_periods(new) > inner_periods(old)`, so **no sentence may be added inside a line.**
  > A swap can build a payoff. A gap cannot — a gap needs the two beats of "build a prediction, then break it", and where only one beat can be struck, the two-beat move cannot be struck.
- **So a conversion is two things** — ① replace the vessel with one that can strike two beats; ② strike them.

## 1. When radical revision is permitted

The default move of entertainment is: fill the weak axis **without breaking expression and restraint**. That is the right move **when the vessel permits the axis**. When the measurement says it does not, the permitted move is larger.

**Radical revision is permitted when all four of these hold.**

| # | condition |
|---|---|
| 1 | the axis was **measured** as not landing — not assumed |
| 2 | the cause was located in **the vessel** (a rule · a record · a design decision), not in the prose |
| 3 | what may move and what may not is **written down first**, read off the work's own design record |
| 4 | after the revision, **the unmoved part is still countable** (§4-③) |

**What radical revision may change** — the vessel (line and sentence structure) · the size of the cast · the density of dialogue · the notes (註) the work carries about itself.
**What it may not** — whatever the work's design record named as 動かさないもの ("what is not moved") · the 内面 (interior) record of the viewpoint character.

> **The safety valve is not surface restraint — it is checkability.** A radical revision whose 内面 did not move a single cell has not touched outside the skeleton, and that can be counted.

## 2. The eleven principles

### 1 — Measure the vessel first

A one-sentence-per-line draft can throw one beat; paragraphing it allows two. ⛔ **But an existing work may already be paragraphed.** Measured — `habits-01`'s 第1話 and 第2話 were already paragraphized by `335bcb6` (2026-09-26, "第五段——段落化"):

| | before (`335bcb6^`) | now |
|---|---|---|
| 第1話 | body 360 lines · prose 349 · **1.00 sent./line** | body **149** · prose **138** · **2.51** |
| 第2話 | body 412 lines · prose 409 · **1.00** | body **181** · prose **178** · **2.33** |

**If so, skip this step.** ⚠ **Always measure before deciding.** "The original is one sentence per line" is a claim to verify by measurement, not from memory — this clause exists because that error was walked into once (§5 of `examples/habits-comedy/構想/変換の作法.md`).

### 2 — Grow dialogue by **rewording and reply**, not by **business**

**Adding business changes the story. Rewording and replying add beats without changing it.**

**Measured** — 第1話: dialogue **11 lines → 62**. **Not one new piece of business.** What grew was rewording of the same business, and replies.

**⚠ Note — a reply need not be spoken.** 第2話's 動かさないもの forbids the viewpoint character to speak, so 第1話's device (「はい。」×30) is unavailable. The reply moved into **the nod**: she nods mid-sentence; the second and third parent ask for **opposite** things and get **the same nod**; the fourth says nothing and is nodded at anyway. **Dialogue 3 lines → 19.** Still not one new piece of business.

**⚠ How far dialogue can grow is set by the original.** 第1話 11→62 (+51 lines) · 第2話 3→19 (+16). **第2話's ceiling is low because the original's own 動かさないもの forbids the viewpoint character to speak.** The added beats went into the prose's procedure instead — which is why its length came out level with the original (**−27 characters**). **Hitting the ceiling is not a defect.**

### 3 — Make repetition a staircase

**Do not repeat the same procedure at the same length.** Put the count on a **downward slope**.

**Measured** — 第1話's five arrivals: colleague (**two exchanges**) → fluorescent light (one) → curtain (one) → wastebasket (one) → **the fifth person (zero)**. The door line likewise: `:49` (a hand-pushed sound · a spring) → `:127` (the spring only) → `:145` (two knocks) → `:163` (a knock). **Do not re-explain the same door four times.**

### 4 — The third time changes the kind

**Measured** — 第1話's chair: 「椅子。」「出てます。」 → 「それ。」「車です。」 → **「椅子。」「行きました。」** — **at the third, the chair actually goes somewhere.** And again: 「棚、空いてます。」→「空いてるうちに。」→「今、空いてます。」→「埋めます。」→「はい。」→ **it is not filled.**

### 5 — Plant and pay off

Put an object or a sound in the first half; return it in the second **from a different place**.

**Measured** — 「鳴るのは、車である。」 planted at `:107`, returned at `:237` **from a different chair**. ⛔ **A payoff is not explained** (principle 6).

### 6 — Do not place 註

**Do not explain the joke's reasoning in the prose.** The reader laughs on the two beats; a 註 (the text's note about itself — the reasoning, spelled out) placed **after** them cancels the laugh.

**Measured — the 註 cut from 第1話**: 「同じ室の者である。名は、知らない。」 (four times, once per arrival) ／ 「車である。」 (four times, once per chair pulled) ／ 「用件が、無い。」 ／ 「通れる。」 ／ 「どれを引いたかは、分からない。」 ／ 「同じ形なので、間違えても、同じである。」 ／ the closing 「私のと、同僚のと、あとの四人のとである。」 ／ 「ゆえに、」 ／ 「両方、片手である。」
第2話 likewise: the 註 on the guests' purposes, and the narrator's explanations of her own not-speaking.

⚠ **A 註 and an attribution are different things** (§4, "Measure before cutting").

### 7 — Make the reader count

**A number belongs to the procedure, not to meaning.** When **the same number comes back counting something else**, that is a beat.

**Measured** — 第1話: **five steps** out and five back / **three** for the creaking board and for the number of knocks / **six** for desks, chairs, and chairs that have left. 第2話: **three** for the ledgers that tilt, for the mouths that open, and for the windows that return.

### 8 — Place a reversal; do not pay it off

**Measured** — the delivery is never completed (「出席簿は、行っていない。」) / chairs reach the shelf **first** / 「埋めます。」 and it is not filled / 「入っていないのが、六つある。」

### 9 — The bustle happens inside the procedure

**Not falls, shouts, or running. It happens among objects and procedures.**

**Measured** — 第1話: the door swings back and hits a shoulder / a chair blocks the aisle and 「私は、椅子と椅子のあいだを通る。」 (**not** 「通れる。」) / all four handles are the same shape.
**⚠ Do not run to fear or to cuteness for it.** A laugh is not bought by frightening the reader or by flattering them.

### 10 — Put nothing in the serious beat

**Never go for a laugh in the episode's serious place.**

**Measured** — 第1話 never goes for a laugh across `:297`–`:329` (the smell of paper · the hand that stops · cold paper · the one line left). **Even with four people added, none was placed in that range.** 第2話: none of the three added parents enters the viewpoint character's 内面.
✅ **This is checkable** — that the 内面 column did not move is the evidence (§4-③).

### 11 — Add no new object

**Increase the number of times an existing object moves, and put people in existing seats.**

**Measured** — every object in 第1話 (chair · handle · step · door · bundle · the spine of the glue · the ring of dust · bag · coat) is an object of the original. **Not one new object.** The four added people sit in **the six seats the original already had** (「机が六つある。椅子が六つある。」 — the room was always built for six; only two had come).

**⚠ Note — count the seats before counting the people.** 第1話's design record named only two people, so the 誰が居るか column itself had to move. **第2話's record says only 「保護者」 and never says one**, so making it four **rewrote no column at all.** **If the record states no number, the record does not move.**

## 3. The eight steps

| # | step | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Measure** | the original's characters · all lines · prose lines · dialogue lines · sentences per line · paragraph-length distribution · forbidden terms. **⚠ Do not start writing without counting.** |
| **1** | **Copy the skeleton** | Copy the design record's columns onto one sheet. **Decide first what moves and what does not.** |
| **2** | **Inspect the vessel** | Principle 1. If it can strike only one beat, paragraph it. **If it is already paragraphed, skip.** |
| **3** | **Count the objects that already exist** | Principle 11. Desks · chairs · steps · doors · windows · ledgers — **find the cards that make a new object unnecessary.** |
| **4** | **Design the two beats** | Principles 3–8. Staircase · the third time · plant and pay off · reversal. **This step is the longest.** |
| **5** | **Write** | Grow dialogue by rewording and reply (principle 2). |
| **6** | **Cut the 註** | Principle 6. **Read afterwards; delete the sentences that explain the reasoning.** |
| **7** | **Check** | The seven of §4. |

## 4. The checks

**① Forbidden terms.** Drive `検査/check.py`'s ⛔ to **0**.
**② Paragraph length.** **Zero paragraphs of five or more sentences** (第1話: 0. The original: 4).
**③ The 内面 column did not move.** — **If people were added and the 内面 column did not shift a single cell, the way they were added did not touch outside the skeleton.** (第1話: not one cell. 第2話: all three values identical to the original.)
**④ No added person inside the serious beat.** **Do not put one added person inside that range.**
**⑤ Count the sentences dropped, by full stop.** Do not count "rewordings" and "deletions" together.
**⑥ Put the 註 ratio beside the original's.** (below)
**⑦ Count whose voice it is.** **Count the quotation marks, and their owner.** 第2話: **19 marks, all 19 the parents'. The viewpoint character has 0.** — "She does not speak" is a fact that shows up in a count.

### ⚠ Measure before cutting

**"It's too explainy" is often a property of the original work.**

**Measured** — in 第1話's conversion, sentences ending in である were **26.8% of the original's prose sentences (93/347) · 19.3% here (79/409)**; sentence length was nearly the same (9.7 · 9.5 characters, the 。 counted). 第2話: 18.6% (77/414) · 16.8% (65/387), sentences 8.8 · 9.0.

**⚠ Correction (2026-10-15).** This clause previously read 「20.8% here」 and gave a second pair — copula endings, 70.0% · 56.9% — which **could not be reproduced** under any definition tried (counting sentences ending in である / だ / です gives 27.4% · 19.6%). Only である末 is stated here, with its denominator. **The claim does not move** — the original's である endings went *down*, so the explainy-ness is the original's property, on that pair alone.

**So what these conversions cut was the 註 (the reasoning) alone.** **Attributions — the origin of an object's weight, coldness, roundness — were kept** (「外の空気のぶんである。」「重さは、一年ぶんである。」).
⚠ **Whether to break the attributions too is a separate ruling.**

## 5. The seven failure modes

| # | failure | why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trying to be funny with vocabulary** | It can throw one beat (§0) |
| 2 | **Trying to be funny with a 註** | A 註 after the two beats cancels the laugh (principle 6) |
| 3 | **Large physical action (falls · shouts · running)** | It sits outside the procedure, and drifts toward fear and cuteness (principle 9) |
| 4 | **Making a new object or a new setting** | It leaves the skeleton (principle 11) |
| 5 | **Touching the serious beat** | The episode's core breaks (principle 10) |
| 6 | **Adding business** | The story changes (principle 2) |
| 7 | **Cutting without measuring** | You delete a property of the original as if it were a defect (§4) |

## How the skills use this

- **entertainment** — reads this when the weak axis is not a weakness of the prose but a **prohibition of the vessel**. Then the revision may be radical, under the four conditions of §1 and the checks of §4.
- **revise-for-reader** — the surface lever (immersion · reread · the shift after reading) is untouched by this; a radical revision still has to keep the promise.
- **plot-design** — the 動かさないもの column of a scene table is what §1-3 reads.

The measurements behind it (第1話 · 第2話, and the case's own record discrepancies) live in `examples/habits-comedy/` — `構想/変換の作法.md` · `構想/各話/`. This file is the thin, citable principle.
